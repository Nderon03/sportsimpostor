# 🏆 Sports Impostor

**Can you spot the fake, or will you get caught bluffing?**

Sports Impostor is a sports-themed social deduction party game where players use clues, strategy, and deception to expose the hidden impostor. Everyone is in on the secret — except the player who has to bluff their way through.

🌐 **Play now:** https://sportsimpostor.netlify.app/

## 🎮 How to Play

1. **Set up your game** — Choose your players, sports category, and available game settings.
2. **Discover your role** — Players take turns viewing their roles privately. Most players receive the secret sports-related answer, while the impostor must work it out without giving themselves away.
3. **Give your clues** — Take turns sharing hints that demonstrate your knowledge without revealing too much.
4. **Discuss and debate** — Analyse everyone's clues, question suspicious answers, and try to identify the impostor.
5. **Vote** — Decide who you believe the impostor is.
6. **Reveal the result** — Discover whether the group caught the impostor or whether they successfully blended in.

Depending on the enabled game settings, an eliminated impostor may have a final opportunity to guess the secret answer and turn the game around.

## ✨ Features

- 🏅 **Sports-themed gameplay** — Built around sports knowledge, clues, bluffing, and social deduction.
- 🕵️ **Secret roles** — Keep players guessing about who knows the answer and who is pretending.
- ⚙️ **Customisable settings** — Configure your game before the round begins.
- 💡 **Impostor hints** — Optional hints help balance the difficulty and give impostors a chance to blend in.
- 🗳️ **Voting and elimination** — Discuss suspicious clues and vote to expose the impostor.
- 🎯 **Optional Final Impostor Guess** — Let a caught impostor attempt to identify the secret answer when this setting is enabled.
- ⏱️ **Suspenseful gameplay** — Countdown sequences, dramatic reveals, and game-related animations make each round more exciting.
- 📱 **Mobile-friendly experience** — Play directly in your browser, including on your phone.
- 🎉 **Made for groups** — Perfect for friends, sports fans, game nights, and competitive gatherings.

*Features and available settings may vary as the game evolves.*

## 🏆 The Objective

**If you're a regular player:** Give convincing clues, identify suspicious behaviour, and work with the group to expose the impostor.

**If you're the impostor:** Listen carefully, read the room, construct believable clues, and avoid being caught. If given the opportunity, use your knowledge of the discussion to guess the secret answer.

The challenge is finding the balance between revealing enough to prove you know the answer and saying too much.
